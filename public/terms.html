07Free service, optional support

Zendo is free to use. There is no subscription and no paywall. Support is voluntary and separate from using the app.

Tips and donations

The app shows an optional QRIS tip panel. Scanning it pays the developer directly from your own banking app. A donation is a gift. It is not a purchase of goods or services, it gives you no extra rights, and it is generally non-refundable. Zendo receives no transaction details from that flow.

Optional Journal Pack unlocks

Some optional Journal Pack unlocks are sold through a payment gateway (Bayar GG). When you choose one, the app sends the pack identifier to a server function, which creates a payment link, and you pay on the gateway's own site. The unlock is granted when the gateway confirms payment by webhook. If demo mode is on, or no gateway key is set, checkout short-circuits to a demo redirect and no money moves.

Zendo never processes or stores your card or bank credentials. Payment details stay with the payment provider.
